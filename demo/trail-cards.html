Trail card style variations

Each row is one style applied to 4 real trails (Half Dome, Mist Trail, Skyline-Paradise, Garfield Peak). The trace lines are real GPS data from the production database, normalized + rendered as SVG. Pick the row whose vibe you like — I'll wire it into app/page.tsx Featured grid and the /trails index.

Style A Minimal Just the trace, on dark. Quietest. Best if the catalog is meant to feel like a dataset.
Style B Topographic Subtle radial contours behind the trace. Whispers "we know the terrain" without screaming.
Style C Difficulty-tinted Trace colored + glowing by difficulty. Adds information density, slightly arcade-y.
Style D Map-sketch Trace floats over a hint of terrain blobs, start/end markers. Closest to "looking at a map".
Style E Poster Magazine cover energy: large display serif, trace as faint accent. Best for the homepage Featured 6.
Style F Poster + difficulty color E's layout, C's glowing colored trace. 4 cards span all 4 difficulty tiers (green/blue/orange/red) so you can see the full palette together.
